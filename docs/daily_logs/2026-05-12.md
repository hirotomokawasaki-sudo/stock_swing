#### 3. 閾値緩和とパラメータ最適化（11:01-11:08）

**Step 1: 30日分の5分足データ追加収集**
```

AAPL:  3,957 bars
```

**Step 2: 閾値緩和テスト**

| Config | Trades | Win Rate | Total Return |
|--------|--------|----------|-------------|
| Original (Strict) | 35 | 77.1% | 58.03% |
| Relaxed VWAP | 35 | 77.1% | 58.03% |
| **Relaxed Momentum** | **49** | **77.6%** | **108.53%** |
| Both Relaxed | 49 | 77.6% | 108.53% |

**発見:**
- VWAP threshold を緩和しても効果なし
- **Momentum threshold を 1.0% → 0.5% に緩和で劇的改善**
  - Trades: 35 → 49 (+40%)
  - Total Return: 58.03% → 108.53% (+87%)

**Step 3: パラメータグリッドサーチ（48組み合わせ）**

**最適パラメータ特定:**
```yaml

Momentum Threshold:  0.3%
Lookback Bars:       25
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Total Trades:        55
Win Rate:            76.4%
Avg Return:          2.88% per trade
Total Return:        158.5%
Sharpe Ratio:        0.43
```

**Top 3 Configurations:**

| Rank | VWAP% | Mom% | Lookback | Win Rate | Total Return |
|------|-------|------|----------|----------|-------------|
| 🥇 1st | 0.5 | 0.3 | 25 | 76.4% | **158.5%** |
| 🥈 2nd | 0.3 | 0.3 | 25 | 75.5% | 147.0% |
| 🥉 3rd | 0.5 | 0.3 | 15 | 76.0% | 138.9% |

**重要な発見:**
1. **VWAP Threshold の影響は限定的** (0.3% ~ 1.0% で結果ほぼ同じ)
2. **Momentum Threshold が最重要** (0.3% が最適)
3. **Lookback Bars = 25 が最適** (最も安定)
