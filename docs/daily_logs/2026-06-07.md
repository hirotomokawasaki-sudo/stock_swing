### Behavior

- Purpose:
  reduce panic-selling into a broad Monday open shock without disabling exits completely.
- Design:
  execution-time filter only; does not modify `SimpleExitV2Strategy` thresholds.
- Default activation:
  active during the first `60` minutes after the Monday US regular open when at least
  `2` of the following signals hit:
  - `SPY gap <= -1.25%`
  - `QQQ gap <= -2.0%`
  - losers ratio `>= 60%`
  - average monitored gap `<= -2.0%`
- Held exit types:
  - `trailing_stop`
  - `breakeven_stop`
  - moderate `stop_loss`
- Forced sell overrides remain:
  - `return_pct <= -12%`
  - single-name gap `<= -15%`
